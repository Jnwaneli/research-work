## Evaluating Distance-Range Compression and Robustness of Deep Mixture Density Networks for Wireless Channel Modeling

**Authors:** Joshua Nwaneli, Micah Josiah Devaraj, Youngmin Lee, and Xiaomin Ma

This folder is reserved for the current manuscript draft and future research-paper materials.

### Current manuscript

Upload the manuscript here using the filename:

`DMDN_Distance_Range_Compression_and_Robustness.docx`

The study evaluates a compressed 297–303 m distance-domain DMDN configuration against a 10–300 m full-range baseline and tests robustness under jointly increased path-loss exponent and Gaussian-noise variance. The compressed configuration achieved OA = 0.982 versus 0.971 for the full-range baseline and reduced observed training time from approximately 22 minutes to 2 minutes per run under the tested environment.
